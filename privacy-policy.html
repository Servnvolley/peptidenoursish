Information We Collect

We collect your phone number solely for the purpose of sending you a one-time verification code to authenticate your account. We do not collect, store, or share your phone number with any third parties for marketing purposes.

How We Use Your Information

Your phone number is used only to:

  • Send a one-time verification code via SMS to confirm your identity
  • Identify your account within the app

SMS Communications

By providing your phone number, you consent to receive a single SMS verification code from PeptideNourish. Message and data rates may apply. You will not receive marketing messages via SMS.

To opt out of SMS, reply STOP to any message. For help, reply HELP.

Data Storage

Your personal profile data (nutrition goals, meal logs, avatar customization) is stored locally on your device using browser local storage. We do not transmit or store this data on our servers.

Third-Party Services

We use Twilio to send SMS verification codes. Twilio's privacy policy can be found at twilio.com/legal/privacy.
