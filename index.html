Reconstitution Guide
💧 Standard Reconstitution Protocol
Use Bacteriostatic Water (BAC water) — not sterile water. BAC water contains 0.9% benzyl alcohol which prevents bacterial growth and extends vial life up to 28 days refrigerated. Never use tap water or saline.
mg per vial
Most providers recommend 1–2 mL for precise dosing. More water = larger injection volume per dose.
📐 Your Reconstitution Calculator
Dose (mcg) Dose (mg) Draw to (IU) Volume
IU = insulin unit markings on a U-100 syringe. 1 IU = 0.01 mL. Always confirm your dose with your prescribing clinician.
⚠️ Injection Tips
Inject BAC water slowly down the side of the vial, never directly onto the powder. Swirl gently — never shake. Let sit 5–10 min if it doesn't fully dissolve. Store reconstituted vial refrigerated at 2–8°C. Use within 28 days.
